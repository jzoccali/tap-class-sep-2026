Twelve weeks

What she watches. What she ships.

Six modules. One Cheval artifact per week. Watching without shipping is skipping class.

Module 1 · Weeks 1-2 · Owner to operator

TAP: orientation, Profitable Modern Practice, Start Smart, Mini Business Plan, “Your Current Model is Keeping You Stuck,” niche sharpen only. Challenge: 30-second elevator pitch (the one already sitting in TAP, unused).

Ship: Walk-away card scored in ink. Cheval pitch said out loud. One-page mini plan for the clinic that exists, not the one she wants. Three owner-dependent loops named with a person who should own each.

Module 2 · Weeks 3-4 · Commitment, not visits

TAP: Patient Commitment Formula, high-retention package framework, customer experience, client journey, intake tools, Intake Conversion System intro.

Ship: Cheval rewritten as a 90-day commitment, not a visit count. Journey map with Mary / Amanda / clinician on it. Discovery / intake offer with a price.

Module 3 · Weeks 5-7 · Packages that sell

TAP: Package Building Blueprint, all five modules. One real package-feedback recording. Menu of services workshop. Susan Federico’s live consult audio.

Ship: Three-tier Cheval stack, priced. Amanda quotes it for seven days with zero Angela overrides. Consult script and one-pager in the room, not in a drive Angela owns.

Module 4 · Weeks 8-9 · Sell without Angela in the room

TAP: Selling with integrity, premium-package psychology, clinical roadmap, Intake Conversion System modules 2 and 3.

Ship: Clinical roadmap for the core package. A close attempted by someone other than Angela, live or recorded. Discovery offer in the first-call script.

Module 5 · Weeks 10-11 · Keep them

TAP: From Packages to Partnerships, all five modules. Optional marketing lessons only if Natalie still cannot post without approval.

Ship: One membership model chosen. One-pager. Five current patients offered it. Yes / no / objection log.

Module 6 · Week 12 · Walk-away exam

TAP: automations masterclass only where follow-up currently dies when Angela is gone. HR docs only if the five-day roster needs them.

Ship: Coverage roster. Dated walk-away score on all seven items. Written GO, PAUSE, or NO-GO on Clinic #2. If three items are not 5, the answer is PAUSE and the gaps are the next semester, not a lease.
